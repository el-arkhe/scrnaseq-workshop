# Bibliografía, guías, post y videos seleccionados

## Bibliografía

- [Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor - OSCA Book](https://github.com/OSCA-source/OSCA) \
Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor” explain users some common workflows for the analysis of single-cell RNA-seq data (scRNA-seq). The book will show you how to make use of cutting-edge Bioconductor tools to process, analyze, visualize, and explore scRNA-seq data. \
Copyright: Bioconductor, 2020


- [Bioconductor OSCA and OSTA books - Prior Art](https://www.sc-best-practices.org/introduction/prior_art.html) \
In comparison to Bioconductor OSCA, this paper and its associated analysis are not constrained by a specific tool ecosystem, offering a broader perspective on the range of topics covered. However, the accompanying example analysis lacks beginner-friendliness and has become outdated. Analogously to Bioconductor OSCA, Lücken & Theis do not address newer developments such as RNA velocity, spatial transcriptomics, or multi-omics. Despite these limitations, we highly recommend this paper as a valuable introduction to the field and as a guide to initial best practices in scRNA-seq analysis. \
Copyright: 2023 \
By Lukas Heumos, Anna Schaar, single-cell best practices consortium \
Journal Citation: Heumos, L., Schaar, A.C., Lance, C. et al. Best practices for single-cell analysis across modalities. Nat Rev Genet 24, 550–572 (2023). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41576-023-00586-w
